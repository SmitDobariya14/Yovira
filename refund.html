Digital Products: YOVIRA primarily sells downloadable digital products. Please carefully review the product description before completing your purchase.

1. Digital Product Policy

Because YOVIRA products are digital products that may be delivered immediately after purchase, all sales are generally considered final once the product has been successfully delivered or made available for download.

Please make sure you have selected the correct product before completing your purchase.

2. When a Refund May Be Considered

Although digital purchases are generally non-refundable, YOVIRA may review certain exceptional situations.

Examples may include:

  • A duplicate payment for the same product.
  • A technical issue that prevents delivery of a purchased product and cannot reasonably be resolved.
  • A transaction that appears to have been processed incorrectly.

Any refund decision will be made based on the circumstances of the individual case and applicable law.

3. Download Problems

If you paid for a product but cannot access your download, please contact us before requesting a refund.

We will first attempt to resolve the delivery problem, which may include providing a new download link or correcting a technical issue.

4. Incorrect Product Selection

Customers are responsible for reviewing the product name, description, features and price before completing payment.

Purchasing the wrong planner, changing your mind after purchase, or deciding that you no longer need the product does not automatically qualify for a refund.

5. Downloading Does Not Mean Automatic Refund Eligibility

Once a digital product has been delivered or made available to the customer, the purchase may not be refundable simply because the product has not yet been opened, used or printed.

6. Refund Request Process

If you believe your situation qualifies for review, contact YOVIRA as soon as possible.

Please include:

  • Your name.
  • The email address used during purchase.
  • Order or transaction information.
  • The product purchased.
  • A clear explanation of the issue.
