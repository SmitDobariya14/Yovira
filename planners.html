Questions

Which planner should I choose?

Choose Essential if you want the core wedding planning system. Choose Ultimate if you want the expanded set of comparison, inspiration, RSVP and wedding-day worksheets.

What is the difference?

Essential focuses on the fundamental planning tools. Ultimate includes everything in Essential plus additional worksheets for deeper planning and organization.

Can I print the planner?

Yes. The planner is designed for printable PDF use.

Can I use it digitally?

Yes. The digital version can be used with compatible PDF applications that support filling PDF forms.

Is this a physical planner?

No. YOVIRA planners are digital products. You receive the applicable digital files after purchase.

Are more planners coming?

Yes. Life and additional planner categories are planned for future YOVIRA releases.
